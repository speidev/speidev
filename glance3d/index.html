// Overview

Open a 3D model. Orbit around it. Close it.

Glance3D is a tiny standalone viewer for glTF and GLB files. There's no editor, no scene panel and no installer: drop a model on the window and look at it. It's about 1,500 lines of C# on a hand-written OpenGL renderer, small enough to read in an afternoon.

yaw −120°
pitch −20° Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · double-click to reset

// Download

One self-contained executable per platform (x64; Apple Silicon on macOS). No .NET install needed. Run glance3d model.glb or drop a file on the window.
The builds aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run (More info → Run anyway). On macOS, clear the quarantine flag with xattr -d com.apple.quarantine glance3d. Release notes.

// Controls

01Left drag orbits the model. Right drag (or Shift + left) pans. The wheel zooms toward the pivot.
02Double-click any surface and the camera orbits around that point from then on, which helps with close inspection.
03Tab switches to fly mode: WASD to move, QE down and up, Shift for speed. Switching back doesn't jump the view.
04F frames the model, R resets the view, Z toggles wireframe, G toggles the floor grid, F11 goes fullscreen.

// Reads

FormatsglTF 2.0Binary .glb and JSON .gltf with external or embedded buffers.
SceneNode hierarchyNode transforms are baked in at load, mirrored instances keep their winding, and triangle strips and fans are expanded.
MaterialsBase colorTexture × factor × vertex color. Alpha mask and blend, double-sided, and KHR_materials_unlit.
Pixel artNEAREST samplersA model that asks for nearest filtering gets it, so palette-textured low-poly art stays crisp.
CharactersSkinsSkinned meshes are posed by their joints and shown in bind pose instead of falling apart at the origin.
Not yetRoadmapAnimation playback, morph targets, PBR metal/roughness, KTX2/WebP textures.
